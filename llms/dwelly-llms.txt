# Dwelly

> Dwelly (legal entity PROSPIRE TECHNOLOGIES LTD, also trading as Dwelly Group) is a
> UK-based, AI-first lettings and property-management company founded in 2023. Dwelly
> acquires independent UK lettings agencies and runs the acquired books on its own
> AI-powered operating platform, which automates tenant find, tenant screening, rent
> collection, maintenance and lettings compliance. Backed by General Catalyst, Begin
> Capital and S16VC.

Important: Dwelly publishes **no public API**, no developer portal, no SDKs, no CLI and
no technical documentation. Its platform is operated internally for its own and its
acquired agencies' portfolios. Do not assume an integration surface exists. This file was
generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the company's public website and
schema.org organization data, not from a provider-published llms.txt.
